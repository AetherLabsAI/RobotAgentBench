Household Memory

Meals and visitors

We spend time together around the coffee table and sofa. The large meal tray is used for dinner at the coffee table; the wooden tray is for refreshments when someone visits. Before sitting down to eat or spend time with a visitor, we put away loose belongings and deal with used packaging in those seating and serving areas.

Alex enjoyed the warm beef meal at our last dinner. He took a knife and a fork with it and asked us to remember that arrangement for next time.

Emma comes home to the leftovers she saved for dinner. She warms them before eating and takes a fork. She asked us to keep that arrangement for her evenings at home.

Ryan asked for the same beef meal and cutlery as Alex. He also had a freshly made coffee with dinner and asked whether we could keep that combination for his next dinner here.

Dinner and its accompanying items are served together on the large meal tray at the coffee table.

On Alex's recent visits, he has gone straight for the Oreos and a Sprite. Before leaving last time, he told us to remember that combination for his next visit.

Emma opened the barbecue Pringles and a Coca-Cola when she last came over. When we arranged another visit, she asked whether we could have the same refreshments again.

Ryan stops by after training. On those visits, he has a Chomps beef stick and the orange Gatorade. He asked us to remember that pairing.

For a visit, we bring out the available packs of that guest's usual refreshments together on the wooden tray. Other packaged refreshments on the shelf are kept as supplies.

Going out

Alex uses the open bag for shopping trips. His final check is for his wallet and water bottle, both packed in the bag.

Emma uses the open bag when she works away from home. She checks for her wallet and power bank before leaving; she relies on her phone throughout the day. After a long afternoon at the library, she started taking her own water as well. She puts her glasses back in their case between reading sessions and keeps the case with the rest of her things in the bag.

Ryan takes his water bottle and glasses case when he goes for a walk in the park. He keeps them together in the open bag.

We like coming home to the seating and serving areas ready to use again. Getting ready to leave includes putting away whatever was left out there and dealing with any used packaging or table stains, following the usual household arrangements below.

Packing the wallet for an outing is different from its everyday storage arrangement.

Where things belong

Books that are still being read belong on the small side table, where they remain easy to reach. The TV remote belongs on the TV cabinet. Caps go on the coat rack.

The green notebook is kept in the coffee-table drawer, with the drawer closed afterward. Outside preparations for an outing, the wallet stays in the storage basket beside the bins. The glasses case, water bottle and power bank are kept in the open bag.

Waste and cleaning

We sort household waste into the appropriate bins in the room. Things that are still being used or kept for later are not rubbish.

When there is a stain on the coffee table, we clean it with the brush and return the brush to where it was kept afterward. A clean table has nothing to scrub. Furniture and storage containers stay in place when the room is tidied.
